Range Bagging Demo

Simulating ecological niche modeling with presence-only data

How it Works:

  1. 1. Blue dots: Species occurrence records
  2. 2. Red ellipse: True niche (unknown in reality)
  3. 3. Bootstrap: Sample 70% of points randomly
  4. 4. Convex hull: Draw boundary around sample
  5. 5. Vote: Repeat 20 times, average predictions
  6. 6. Heatmap: Areas with high votes = predicted niche

Key Insight:

Even though each bootstrap sample creates an imperfect boundary (orange), averaging many samples (blue heatmap) provides a robust estimate of the true niche (red dashed ellipse). Areas voted "inside" by many hulls are confidently predicted as suitable habitat. This demonstrates how ensemble methods can overcome the limitations of individual models and reduce overfitting in species distribution modeling.
