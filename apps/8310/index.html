Population Ecology (8310)

Explore the dynamics of populations and communities through mathematical models, ecological theory, and practical applications. This course covers population growth, species interactions, community structure, and ecosystem dynamics.

Course Lectures

1

Fundamental concepts in population ecology, defining populations, and the scope of ecological study.
