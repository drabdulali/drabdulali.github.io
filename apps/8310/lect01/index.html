 {/* Density Dependence: Positive vs Negative (Qualitative) */}

Negative Density Dependence

  • Competition reduces births or increases deaths as N increases.
  • Predator attraction or disease transmission may increase with N.

Positive Density Dependence (Allee effect)

  • At very low N, cooperation or mate-finding increases growth.
  • Can create an unstable equilibrium at low N.
