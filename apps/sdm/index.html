Theory

Mathematical Framework

MaxEnt: Maximum Entropy Distribution

Objective: Find distribution \(\hat{\pi}\) that maximizes entropy subject to feature constraints:

$$\hat{\pi} = \arg\max_{\pi} \left[ -\sum_{x} \pi(x) \ln \pi(x) \right] \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \mathbb{E}_{\hat{\pi}}[f_j] = \mathbb{E}_{\tilde{\pi}}[f_j], \; \forall j$$

Gibbs Distribution (solution):

$$q_\lambda(x) = \frac{e^{\lambda \cdot f(x)}}{Z_\lambda}, \quad Z_\lambda = \sum_{x} e^{\lambda \cdot f(x)}$$

\(\pi(x)\)
Probability of species presence at location \(x\) across the landscape
\(f_j(x)\)
Feature function \(j\) (e.g., temperature, precipitation) evaluated at \(x\)
\(\lambda_j\)
Weight for feature \(j\), learned from occurrence data via regularized fitting
\(Z_\lambda\)
Normalizing constant ensuring probabilities sum to 1
\(\tilde{\pi}\)
Empirical distribution of features at occurrence locations
\(H(\pi) = -\sum \pi \ln \pi\)
Shannon entropy: measure of distribution uniformity / uncertainty

BIOCLIM: Percentile Envelope

Suitability Score:

$$S_{\text{BIOCLIM}}(x) = \frac{2}{d}\sum_{j=1}^{d}\left(\min\big(P_j(x),\; 1 - P_j(x)\big)\right)$$

where \(P_j(x)\) is the percentile rank of variable \(j\) at location \(x\) relative to occurrence records, and \(d\) is the number of environmental variables.

Model Evaluation Metrics

AUC (Area Under ROC Curve):

$$\text{AUC} = \int_0^1 \text{TPR}(t)\; d\text{FPR}(t) = P(\hat{s}_+ > \hat{s}_-)$$

Probability that a randomly chosen presence site is scored higher than a randomly chosen absence site.

TSS (True Skill Statistic):

$$\text{TSS} = \text{Sensitivity} + \text{Specificity} - 1 = \frac{TP}{TP+FN} + \frac{TN}{TN+FP} - 1$$

Key Insight: Why SDMs Matter for Conservation

Species Distribution Models are essential tools for predicting how species ranges shift under climate change, identifying priority areas for conservation, and understanding the ecological requirements of species. By combining occurrence records with environmental layers (temperature, precipitation, elevation), SDMs estimate the fundamental niche of a species. MaxEnt has become the gold standard for presence-only modeling due to its strong theoretical foundation and consistent performance. However, ensemble approaches that combine multiple algorithms often outperform any single method, providing more robust predictions for management decisions.
