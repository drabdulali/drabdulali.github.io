Key Patterns Revealed:
→ Highest Transboundary Potential: Diseases with multi-species host range + wildlife reservoirs + multiple transmission routes (AI, FMD, ND)
→ Vector-borne Advantage: 6/8 vector-borne diseases achieve 3+ continent spread vs 3/7 direct transmission diseases
→ Wildlife Involvement Critical: 13/15 TADs involve wildlife; 11/13 with wildlife reservoirs achieve high economic impact
→ Zoonotic Amplification: 4/4 zoonotic diseases have multi-species host ranges and wildlife involvement
→ RNA Virus Dominance: 9/11 RNA viruses vs 2/3 DNA viruses achieve global (5-continent) distribution
