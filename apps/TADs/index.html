About Transboundary Animal Diseases

What are TADs?

Transboundary Animal Diseases (TADs) are highly contagious epidemic diseases that can spread rapidly across national borders, causing significant socioeconomic impacts and threatening food security.

Key Characteristics

  • High morbidity and/or mortality rates
  • Potential for rapid spread
  • Major economic consequences
  • International trade implications
  • Many are zoonotic

Examples of TADs

  • Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD)
  • Highly Pathogenic Avian Influenza
  • African Swine Fever
  • Peste des Petits Ruminants
  • Rift Valley Fever
  • Lumpy Skin Disease

Research Focus

Understanding the past emergence patterns, current distribution, and future risks of TADs is crucial for developing effective surveillance and control strategies.
