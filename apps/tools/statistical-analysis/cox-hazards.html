Interpretation

How to Interpret Hazard Ratios

HR > 1: Increased hazard of reemergence (risk factor)
HR < 1: Decreased hazard of reemergence (protective factor)
HR = 1: No effect on hazard

Example: HR = 1.41 means 41% higher hazard of reemergence for each 1-unit increase in the covariate (or 1-SD increase if standardized).
