Kaplan-Meier Survival Curve Generator

Visualize time-to-reemergence with survival curves, confidence intervals, and statistical comparisons between groups.

Data Input

S(t) = Product[(n_i - d_i) / n_i]

Format: time (months), event (1=reemergence, 0=censored), group

Interpretation Guide

Survival Probability S(t): Probability of remaining disease-free at time t.

Censored: Observations where reemergence was not observed (study ended, lost to follow-up).

Log-Rank Test: Compares survival distributions between groups. p < 0.05 suggests significant difference.

Median Survival: Time at which 50% of units have experienced reemergence.
