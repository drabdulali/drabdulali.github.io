Proportional Hazards Assumption

The Cox model assumes hazard ratios are constant over time. Schoenfeld residuals help test this assumption:

  • No pattern: PH assumption satisfied
  • Trend over time: PH violation
  • p < 0.05: Significant violation

Interpretation

The Schoenfeld residuals plot shows the relationship between scaled residuals and time. A flat LOESS line suggests the proportional hazards assumption is satisfied. A significant trend (upward or downward) indicates the hazard ratio changes over time, violating the assumption.
