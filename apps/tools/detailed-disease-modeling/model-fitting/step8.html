8 Confidence Intervals

Confidence Intervals

Quantify uncertainty in \(\hat\beta\), \(\hat\gamma\), \(\hat{k}\), and \(\hat{\mathcal{R}}_0\) using four complementary methods.

Is Step 8 just for \(\beta\)?

No. Profile likelihood CIs are computed separately for every parameter of interest: \(\beta\), \(\gamma\), the dispersion \(k\), and the derived quantity \(\mathcal{R}_0\). Each profiling involves fixing one parameter, re-optimizing over all others, and tracing the resulting log-likelihood curve.

⚠

Wide CI does NOT mean refix the parameter

A wide profile likelihood CI means your data does not contain enough information to pin down that parameter precisely. This is an honest statement of uncertainty. Fixing it arbitrarily to a literature value would give artificially narrow CIs and biased estimates. Instead: report the uncertainty, collect more data, or use informative priors (Bayesian approach).
