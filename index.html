Welcome

Welcome to my academic portfolio! I'm a PhD student in Ecology with a focus on infectious disease dynamics and epidemiology. This website serves as both my professional portfolio and a comprehensive study resource for my doctoral work.

Research Focus

Investigating the ecological and evolutionary dynamics of infectious diseases in natural populations, with emphasis on host-pathogen interactions and disease transmission patterns.

Academic Journey

Interactive Learning

About Me

I am a PhD student passionate about understanding the complex dynamics of infectious diseases in ecological systems. My work bridges ecology, epidemiology, and evolutionary biology to address fundamental questions about disease transmission, host-pathogen coevolution, and the ecological factors that drive disease outbreaks.
