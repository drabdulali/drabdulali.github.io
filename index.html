Exploring Research in Disease Ecology

Combining ecological theory with mathematical modeling to understand and predict disease dynamics.

Research Focus

As a PhD student in infectious disease ecology, I investigate the complex interactions between pathogens, hosts, and their environments. My research integrates ecology, and mathematical modeling to understand how diseases emerge, spread, and can be controlled.

My current work focuses on Transboundary Animal Diseases (TADs), exploring their Past, Present, and Future. This research contributes to both fundamental science and public health preparedness through the One Health approach.

Research

Current Research Areas

Investigating fundamental questions in disease ecology and epidemiology.

01

Disease Transmission Networks

Analyzing how contact patterns and network structure influence the spread of infectious diseases in wildlife and human populations. Using network theory to identify superspreaders and optimize intervention strategies.

02

Host-Pathogen Coevolution

Studying the evolutionary dynamics between hosts and pathogens, including virulence evolution, resistance mechanisms, and the ecological factors that shape these coevolutionary relationships.

03

Environmental Drivers of Disease

Investigating how climate change, habitat fragmentation, and anthropogenic disturbance influence disease emergence and transmission dynamics in ecological communities.

04

Zoonotic Disease Spillover

Examining the ecological and evolutionary factors that facilitate pathogen spillover from wildlife to humans, with implications for pandemic preparedness and One Health approaches.

05

Mathematical Epidemiology

Developing and analyzing mathematical models to understand epidemic dynamics, predict outbreak trajectories, and evaluate the effectiveness of public health interventions.

06

Wildlife Disease Surveillance

Designing optimal surveillance strategies for detecting and monitoring infectious diseases in wildlife populations, integrating field methods with statistical modeling approaches.
